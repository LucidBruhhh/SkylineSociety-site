Privacy

This site has no accounts, analytics scripts or upload service. The build-story tool prepares a text download on your device. The form is not submitted to a server and its contents are not stored after you leave the page.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses for security and service operation. Facebook is a separate service with its own policies. Contact Skyline Society through the official Facebook page with questions about this site.

About this site

BRUH’s build thread includes owner-supplied specifications. Other records remain photographic features; missing details are marked as unconfirmed. No membership payments or purchases are processed here.
